Europejska Akademia Lotnictwa · EASA framework

A school, platform, and community for the people who keep European aviation flying.

Europejska Akademia Lotnictwa (EAL) builds structured certification pathways toward Aircraft Maintenance License, digital maintenance tools, and professional upskilling across Europe — from mechanics and MROs to flight schools and engineers.

European ecosystem

Connecting the European aviation industry

An integrated platform for mechanics, engineers, instructors, Maintenance Repair Overhaul (MRO) facilities, and flight schools — from first steps in aviation to a full Aircraft Maintenance License.

What we're building

Core platform modules

01 // CERTPathway

Certification pathways

A clear, structured route from zero to the Aircraft Maintenance License (AML). We support mechanics who already hold Certificates of Recognition (CoR) from accredited EASA-approved partners.

Module: AML-Navigator[EASA framework]
02 // DIGITALSoftware

Digital tools for maintenance

Modern, practical software for maintenance operations. Tools that match the reality of the hangar — smarter workflows, better documentation, and integrations that work for engineers.

Module: Hangar-OS[Digital workflows]
03 // TALENTUpskilling

Talent and education

Programs for new candidates and upskilling for experienced mechanics. From foundational training to advanced specialisation, building confident, competent engineers ready for real-world operations.

Module: Skill-Calibration[Foundational & advanced]
04 // NETWORKCommunity

Aviation community network

A network for the people who keep aircraft safe and serviceable. We connect mechanics, engineers, instructors, and MROs in a shared space for knowledge, opportunities, and professional growth.

Module: Net-Connect[Pan-European]
